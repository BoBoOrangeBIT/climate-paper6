## Fig. 1

**Figure 1.** Equivalent temperature reduction of fans and evaporative coolers on the SET scale compared with the air conditioning rules. (a) Fan equivalent reduction ΔT at 1.2 m s⁻¹ as a function of outdoor air temperature and relative humidity. The white vertical lines mark the Morris 2021 fan window upper bounds at 37, 38 and 39 °C and the hatched area marks the Foster 2021 exclusion (35 °C and above with relative humidity below 50 %). The white dashed curve is the zero contour above which the fan adds heat. At 30 °C and 50 % relative humidity the fan is worth 3.6 K <!-- R:K010 -->. (b) Evaporative cooler equivalent reduction ΔT at saturation efficiency ε = 0.75 on the same grid. The orange line is the evaporative window boundary (ΔT above 0 and supply air relative humidity at or below 80 %). At 35 °C and 20 % relative humidity the cooler is worth 11.2 K <!-- R:K020 -->. (c) ΔT against outdoor temperature for the air conditioning Default rule (max(T − 23, 0)), the DLS rule (max(T − 26, 0)), the fan at 1.2 m s⁻¹ and the evaporative cooler at ε = 0.75. Colour denotes technology and line style denotes relative humidity (20, 50 and 80 %). (d) Fan ΔT at 50 % relative humidity for 0.8, 1.2 and 2.0 m s⁻¹ (solid lines) and the core temperature variant at 1.2 m s⁻¹ (dashed line). Grey shading marks negative ΔT where the fan heats the body and the thin vertical lines repeat the 37, 38 and 39 °C window bounds. All values are deterministic outputs of the Gagge two node model lookup tables (results/derived/lookup_tables.npz, lookup_fan.csv, lookup_evap.csv) for a standard person at rest, so no uncertainty band is shown. Source data are in figures/source-data/fig1.csv.
