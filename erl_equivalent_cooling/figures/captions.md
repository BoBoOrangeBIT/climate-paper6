# Figure captions

## Fig. 1

**Figure 1.** Equivalent temperature reduction of fans and evaporative coolers on the SET scale compared with the air conditioning rules. (a) Fan equivalent reduction ΔT at 1.2 m s⁻¹ as a function of outdoor air temperature and relative humidity. The white vertical lines mark the Morris 2021 fan window upper bounds at 37, 38 and 39 °C and the hatched area marks the Foster 2021 exclusion (35 °C and above with relative humidity below 50 %). The white dashed curve is the zero contour above which the fan adds heat. At 30 °C and 50 % relative humidity the fan is worth 3.6 K <!-- R:K010 -->. (b) Evaporative cooler equivalent reduction ΔT at saturation efficiency ε = 0.75 on the same grid. The orange line is the evaporative window boundary (ΔT above 0 and supply air relative humidity at or below 80 %). At 35 °C and 20 % relative humidity the cooler is worth 11.2 K <!-- R:K020 -->. (c) ΔT against outdoor temperature for the air conditioning Default rule (max(T − 23, 0)), the DLS rule (max(T − 26, 0)), the fan at 1.2 m s⁻¹ and the evaporative cooler at ε = 0.75. Colour denotes technology and line style denotes relative humidity (20, 50 and 80 %). (d) Fan ΔT at 50 % relative humidity for 0.8, 1.2 and 2.0 m s⁻¹ (solid lines) and the core temperature variant at 1.2 m s⁻¹ (dashed line). Grey shading marks negative ΔT where the fan heats the body and the thin vertical lines repeat the 37, 38 and 39 °C window bounds. All values are deterministic outputs of the Gagge two node model lookup tables (results/derived/lookup_tables.npz, lookup_fan.csv, lookup_evap.csv) for a standard person at rest, so no uncertainty band is shown. Source data are in figures/source-data/fig1.csv.

## Fig. 2

**Figure 2.** Global application of the equivalent cooling framework to ERA5 hourly data for 2020 on a 0.5° land grid. (a) Exposure degree hours removed per year by air conditioning under the Default operating rule (10³ K h per year, colour scale capped at the 99th percentile). (b) Share of Default air conditioning hours that fall inside the fan window (air temperature below 38 °C), which reaches 95.8% <!-- R:K032 --> of all air conditioning hours over land. (c) Fan equivalence ratio, exposure reduction of a 1.2 m s⁻¹ fan divided by that of Default air conditioning, 60.6% <!-- R:K033 --> when aggregated over land. (d) Evaporative cooler equivalence ratio (ε 0.75 over Default air conditioning), 72.8% <!-- R:K051 --> aggregated over land, with values at or above 1 on 44% <!-- R:K052 --> of the land area shown. Ratios in (c) and (d) use a diverging scale centred at 1 (equal exposure reduction to air conditioning) and are capped at 2. Panels (b) to (d) are masked (light grey) where Default air conditioning runs for fewer than 100 hours per year, which leaves 76% <!-- R:K030 --> of the land area, and white denotes ocean. Equirectangular (plate carrée) projection, 60°S to 85°N. Values are single year point estimates without an uncertainty range. Source data in figures/source-data/fig2.csv.

**Figure 3.** Cities and the fan plus air-conditioning combination. (a) Exposure degree hours removed per year in 12 cities (thousand K h) by air conditioning under the Default rule (black) and the DLS rule (grey), by a fan at 1.2 m/s inside the 38 °C window (blue) and by an evaporative cooler with ε 0.75 under the RH_s ≤ 80 % cap (orange), all evaluated over the Default air-conditioning hours. Whiskers span the fan speeds 0.8 to 2.0 m/s and the effectiveness values ε 0.6 to 0.85, each expressed as the sensitivity equivalence ratio times the Default air-conditioning exposure removal. Fans remove 30 % of the air-conditioning exposure in Phoenix <!-- R:K202 --> and 27 % in Riyadh <!-- R:K212 -->, rising to 75 % in Singapore <!-- R:K272 --> and 80 % in Miami <!-- R:K292 -->, whereas evaporative coolers reach 111 % in Phoenix <!-- R:K203 --> and 150 % in Tehran <!-- R:K233 --> but only 2 % in Miami <!-- R:K293 --> and 0 % in Singapore <!-- R:K273 -->. (b) Air-conditioning operating hours per year under the Default rule (black circles) and the DLS rule (grey squares), with the green marker showing the hours that remain once a 3 K fan shift is applied before air conditioning starts. The shift removes 12 % of Default hours in Phoenix <!-- R:K204 --> and 36 % in Singapore <!-- R:K274 -->, against 34.4 % of global land air-conditioning hours <!-- R:K070 -->. (c) Global land coverage (open circles) and equivalence ratio (filled circles) under the Default rule for fan variants (blue) and evaporative variants (orange). The fan equivalence ratio moves from 56.6 % with a 35 °C window <!-- R:K080 --> to 61.6 % with a 39 °C window <!-- R:K082 -->, is 56.7 % with the Foster rule <!-- R:K083 -->, 60.4 % when negative ΔT is counted outside the window <!-- R:K086 -->, and spans 50.3 % <!-- R:K088 --> to 72.4 % <!-- R:K089 --> for 0.8 and 2.0 m/s. The evaporative ratio is 48.4 % with RH_s ≤ 70 % <!-- R:K090 -->, 95.9 % with no humidity cap <!-- R:K091 -->, 87.4 % with the T_wb ≤ 24 °C rule <!-- R:K092 -->, 64.9 % for ε 0.6 <!-- R:K093 -->, 54.3 % for ε 0.85 <!-- R:K094 --> and 92.7 % with a 0.5 m/s airstream <!-- R:K095 -->. Data from results/derived/cities.csv and results/derived/sensitivity.csv for the year 2020. Whiskers in (a) are parameter ranges and not statistical confidence intervals.
