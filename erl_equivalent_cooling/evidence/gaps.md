## 3. The three conflicts the writer must handle explicitly

1. **Fan harm in hot-dry air and in older adults versus the model-based window.** morris2021electric and tartarini2021application give 38–39 °C (and mihalcin2025examining above 43 °C for young adults in dry air), while oconnor2024effect (no benefit at 36 °C), chaseling2025thermal (+0.3 °C at 45 °C and 15 % RH), foster2021quantifying (ineffective at ≥ 35 °C below 50 % RH) and meade2024critical (keep 35 °C, especially 65 and over) point the other way. Resolution for the paper: keep 38 °C as the primary window because it is the published older-adult threshold, show 35 °C and the Foster rule as sensitivities, run the negative-ΔT case, and say in Limitations that the window is external to SET and uncertain for older adults.

2. **SET humidity sensitivity versus weak humidity effects in epidemiology.** The equivalence scale (SET) makes ΔT depend on humidity, and li2018modified says SET still misjudges humidity in hot-humid air, whereas armstrong2019role (445 cities, humidity term lowers mortality 1.1 % per 23 % RH) and lo2023optimal (dry-bulb performs as well as humidity metrics) find little humidity signal in mortality. baldwin2023humidity frames this as unresolved. Resolution: state that humidity enters only through the equivalence step and the window, that the exposure–response function is left generic, and that the DLS and Default humidity setpoints affect only the SET-consistent variant.

3. **Parameter provenance, ε range and DLS setpoint.** No source states ε = 0.6–0.85 (closest: 65–80 % in lo2023optimal full text, 80 % in naderi2020simulation, measured values in camargo2005experimental and harby2019investigation not accessible), and no accessible source states a 26 °C, 60 % RH decent-living setpoint (kikstra2021decent and mastrucci2019improving give the concept only). Resolution: present both as author assumptions bounded by the cited ranges, and ask the author to confirm the DLS source before Methods is drafted. Related smaller items: the 1.2 m/s "with occupant control" clause of ASHRAE 55 and the arens2009moving bibliographic record need checking against the originals.
