## Figure 2

**Figure 2.** Global application of the equivalent cooling framework to ERA5 hourly data for 2020 on a 0.5° land grid. (a) Exposure degree hours removed per year by air conditioning under the Default operating rule (10³ K h per year, colour scale capped at the 99th percentile). (b) Share of Default air conditioning hours that fall inside the fan window (air temperature below 38 °C), which reaches 95.8% <!-- R:K032 --> of all air conditioning hours over land. (c) Fan equivalence ratio, exposure reduction of a 1.2 m s⁻¹ fan divided by that of Default air conditioning, 60.6% <!-- R:K033 --> when aggregated over land. (d) Evaporative cooler equivalence ratio (ε 0.75 over Default air conditioning), 72.8% <!-- R:K051 --> aggregated over land, with values at or above 1 on 44% <!-- R:K052 --> of the land area shown. Ratios in (c) and (d) use a diverging scale centred at 1 (equal exposure reduction to air conditioning) and are capped at 2. Panels (b) to (d) are masked (light grey) where Default air conditioning runs for fewer than 100 hours per year, which leaves 76% <!-- R:K030 --> of the land area, and white denotes ocean. Equirectangular (plate carrée) projection, 60°S to 85°N. Values are single year point estimates without an uncertainty range. Source data in figures/source-data/fig2.csv.
